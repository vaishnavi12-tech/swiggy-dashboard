# swiggy-dashboard
I’m excited to share a project I recently wrapped up: a comprehensive Swiggy Revenue & Delivery Overview dashboard built entirely in Power BI using raw data prepared and cleaned in Excel.
By analyzing key delivery and customer metrics, this interactive dashboard turns messy operational data into clear, visual business intelligence.
📊 Key Features & Insights:
Core KPIs: Monitored 9K total orders, 3M in sales, and tracked an average customer rating of 3.66.
Geographical Performance: Mapped sales volumes across major hubs like Mumbai, Kolkata, and Chennai, identifying Mumbai as the highest revenue generator.
Operational Efficiency: Analyzed delivery time variations (averaging 54 minutes) against city layouts to pinpoint potential bottlenecks.
Consumer Behavior: Cleaned and segmented data for over 8.6K food types and 8.6K unique restaurants to see what drives customer satisfaction.
💡 Key Learnings From This Project:
End-to-End ETL Processes: I learned how to seamlessly connect Excel datasets into Power BI, building an efficient workflow from raw data extraction to final dashboard publishing.
Balancing Scale with Granularity: I learned how to display high-level executive KPIs alongside highly granular operational breakdowns (such as specific delivery time minimums per city).
Identifying Operational Bottlenecks: By tracking both average delivery times (54 mins) and maximum outliers (109 mins), I learned how to isolate operational inefficiencies and understand where logistics might be failing.
Designing for User Experience: I practiced visual layout hierarchy—ensuring the most critical aggregate figures are pinned directly to the top to guide the viewer's eyes naturally downward.
🛠️ Key Skills Applied:

Data Cleaning & Transformation (Excel / Power Query): Removing duplicates, formatting dates, handling blank values, and reshaping unorganized food delivery text data.
Data Visualization & Dashboard Design (Power BI): Choosing appropriate chart types (donut charts, funnel charts, dual-axis line charts) and implementing a clean dashboard layout.
Business Performance Metrics (KPIs): Tracking operational metrics like Total Sales, Order Volume, Average Delivery Time, and Customer Satisfaction (CSAT).
Data Storytelling: Translating dense numerical tables into an intuitive, high-level business narrative for stakeholders.
